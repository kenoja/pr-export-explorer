What does Puerto Rico export?

Every bubble is one product in one year. Bubble area = export value. Click a bubble, or search for a product, to follow it through time in both charts.

1. Exports by industry

Industries are sorted by their total exports, largest first. Each row holds the products of one industry.

2. Exports by product complexity (PCI)

Higher = the product needs more know-how to make. Use the buttons to show only some industries, or only products in which Puerto Rico is competitive (RCA ≥ 1).

Sources. Puerto Rico exports: Puerto Rico Institute of Statistics (IEPR), detailed external trade database, based on U.S. Census Bureau records. World trade and Product Complexity Index: Atlas of Economic Complexity, Harvard Growth Lab (HS92, 4-digit products).

Notes. Values in current US$. "U.S." = shipments to the United States (including the U.S. Virgin Islands, under 0.5% of the value), which are not exports in U.S. trade statistics. RCA compares Puerto Rico's export basket with world trade; with "Foreign countries" it uses foreign exports only, otherwise all destinations. Product-years below $0.1M are left out.
